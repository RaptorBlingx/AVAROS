# AVAROS voice command reference

Replace `Line-1` and `Line-2` with configured asset names.

## Status and help

- “What can you do?”
- “Show the active profile.”
- “List the configured assets.”
- “Show system status.”

## Energy

- “What is the energy per unit for Line-1?”
- “Show total energy this week.”
- “What was the peak demand today?”
- “Show peak tariff exposure.”

## Production

- “What is the OEE for Line-1?”
- “Show throughput this week.”
- “What is the cycle time?”
- “Show changeover time.”

## Material and quality

- “What is the scrap rate for Line-1?”
- “Show the rework rate.”
- “What is material efficiency?”
- “Show recycled content.”

## Carbon

- “What is CO2 per unit?”
- “Show total CO2 this month.”
- “What is CO2 per batch?”

## Supplier

- “Show Supplier-A lead time.”
- “What is Supplier-A defect rate?”
- “Show on-time delivery.”
- “What is supplier CO2 per kilogram?”

## Analysis

- “Compare Line-1 and Line-2 on energy per unit.”
- “Rank all lines by OEE last month.”
- “Give me a factory summary for all lines last month.”
- “Show the scrap rate trend for last month.”
- “What does OEE mean?”
- “Is OEE good on Line-1?”
- “Why did OEE change on Line-1?”
- “What if scrap rate drops by 10 percent on Line-1?”
- “Are there any production anomalies?”
- “Is energy per unit drifting on Line-1?”

Anomaly, drift, and forecast commands require PREVENTION. Performance judgments require a configured objective. Process-parameter scenarios require a configured model. AVAROS is advisory and read-only.
